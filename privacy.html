隐私与使用说明

云上麦加 · Cloud Mecca · 更新于 2026 年 11 月

这是什么

云上麦加是一个线上的灵修与学习空间：查看礼拜时间与朝向、赞念计数、杜阿墙、阅读古兰经，以及学习副朝与朝觐的步骤。3D 庭院只是示意，并非禁寺的复刻。

不能代替真实的功修

这里的一切——包括「朝觐演练」中的巡游与奔走——都是学习与演练，不能代替真实的礼拜、朝觐或副朝，也不构成任何教法裁决。礼拜时间按天文公式计算，请以当地清真寺公布的时间为准；具体教法细节请请教学者。

付费内容

「朝觐演练完整引导课」是付费的学习内容；「支持本站」是对服务器与开发的支持。二者都不是天课（扎卡特）或施舍（索德格），也不会被转交给任何慈善机构。付款通过 PayPal 完成，本站不保存任何支付信息。测试期间在线支付关闭，相关内容免费。

账号

浏览、看礼拜时间、赞念、读经、阿敏不需要账号。发起或加入赞念会、完整引导课需要注册。我们保存：邮箱、显示名称、经过不可逆加密（bcrypt）的密码，以及赞念次数、参加集体赞念次数、完成演练次数等统计数字。你可以选择是否显示你的名字。

位置

点「使用我的位置」时，浏览器会询问你是否允许。位置只在你的设备上用于计算礼拜时间与朝向，保存在本机浏览器里，不会上传到我们的服务器。指南针数据同样只在本机使用。

哪些内容会发送到服务器

  • 你在庭院里的位置与姿态：实时发给同时在线的访客，用来显示你的身影（如你选择显示名字，也会附上显示名称）。不保存。
  • 赞念次数：只记录数字（例如「赞主清净 33 次」），用于每日汇总；登录后会计入你的统计。
  • 杜阿墙内容：经人工审核后匿名公开。为防止刷屏，我们保存一个经过加盐处理、无法还原的网络地址摘要。
  • 古兰经：经文与译文从 AlQuran.cloud 读取，你的设备会直接连接该服务。

Privacy & terms

Cloud Mecca · updated November 2026

What this is

Cloud Mecca is an online space for remembrance and learning: prayer times and qibla, a dhikr counter, a du’a wall, Qur’an reading, and the steps of Umrah and Hajj. The 3D courtyard is illustrative, not a replica of the Sacred Mosque.

Not a substitute for worship

Everything here — including the ṭawāf and saʿy in “Hajj practice” — is learning and practice. It does not replace real prayer, Hajj or Umrah and is not a religious ruling. Prayer times are calculated astronomically; follow your local mosque’s timetable and ask scholars about rulings.

Paid content

The “full guided course” is paid learning content; “Support us” supports hosting and development. Neither is zakat or sadaqah, and neither is passed on to a charity. Payments are handled by PayPal; we never store payment details. During the beta, online payment is switched off and these are free.

Accounts

Browsing, prayer times, dhikr, reading and saying “Āmīn” need no account. Hosting or joining dhikr circles and the full course need one. We store your email, display name, a one-way hashed (bcrypt) password, and simple counts (dhikr, circles joined, practices completed). You choose whether your name is shown.

Location

When you tap “Use my location” your browser asks for permission. Your location is used only on your device to calculate prayer times and qibla, is kept in your browser, and is never uploaded to our server. Compass data stays on your device too.

What is sent to the server

  • Your position and posture in the courtyard, broadcast live to other visitors so they see your figure (with your display name if you choose). Not stored.
  • Dhikr counts — numbers only, for the daily total; when logged in they count toward your stats.
  • Du’a wall posts, published anonymously after manual review. To prevent spam we store a salted, irreversible digest of your network address.
  • Qur’an text and translations are loaded from AlQuran.cloud; your device connects to it directly.

Deleting your account

To delete your account and all data, email 289021604@qq.com.
